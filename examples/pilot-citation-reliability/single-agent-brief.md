# Single-agent condition: research brief

Disposition: complete for the bounded document question; comparative model reliability remains unestablished. This condition was drafted and reviewed by the same Codex assistant in one chat; it is self-review.

RAMP can validate declared constraints and trace a claim to recorded passages. It should not equate that result with a judgment that the passage supports the claim [C-004]. The initial assertion that schemas cannot check any semantics was too broad: defined formats are a counterexample [C-001]. Provenance helps organize assessment [C-002], including assessment of the provenance record itself [C-003]. Evidence IDs and exact source sections appear in evidence.json and sources.json.

Preserve three qualifications in every handoff: format assertions vary by implementation; the two W3C sources are not independent corroborations; this pilot is not a benchmark of general agent accuracy. The final recommendation is an inference, not a quoted theorem. Human source-support review has not been performed.

Open questions: How often do agents preserve qualifications across unrelated topics? Does an independent human reviewer agree with the source-support judgments? Would a blinded, repeated comparison show a practical benefit?
