# Evidence ledger

| Evidence ID | Source URL/path | Title / author / publisher | Published | Accessed | Exact location | Supported claim | Type | Limitations / contrary evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use stable evidence IDs such as E-001. Use ISO dates when known; explicitly mark unknown metadata. Type is observation, quotation, inference, or hypothesis. For an inference, reference the underlying evidence IDs and reasoning. Do not add placeholder rows that imply evidence was gathered.
