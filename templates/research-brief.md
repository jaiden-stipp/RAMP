# Research brief

## Disposition

Complete, partial, or blocked, with the acceptance criteria met and unmet.

## Question and scope

State the question, relevant boundaries, and audience.

## Answer

Give the supported answer and cite evidence IDs for material claims.

## Evidence and reasoning

Link sources with precise locators. Separate observations, inferences, and hypotheses. Explain why the evidence supports the conclusion.

## Contrary evidence and limitations

Describe disagreements, source weaknesses, uncertainty, and alternative explanations.

## Review

Record checks performed, unresolved review issues, and whether review was independent.

## Open questions and next steps

Identify remaining work and an owner when applicable.

## Source ledger

Link the evidence ledger and other artifacts. Do not manufacture content to fill sections.
